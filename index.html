Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the WU Vienna University of Economics and Business, where I am also employed as a Teaching and Research Associate at the Department of Economics.

My research lies at the intersection of macroeconometrics and machine learning. My interests include applied econometrics, forecasting, monetary policy and business cycle analysis.
